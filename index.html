Web app | Working product

ResellerOS

An AI-powered web app for online resellers: generate a listing in seconds, see real profit after cost and fees, and get nudged to drop the price on stale stock. It began as a Native.builder hackathon build (August 2026) and has since grown into a designed, documented product.

Dark green sidebar on a calm cream canvas, with gold used as a fill under dark text after my contrast audit showed gold text failed. Landing page, authentication set and four app screens live in one design system.

Data model covers users, items, listings, drafts, sales and watchlist entries. Listing generation is an editable AI draft, profit is calculated from cost, fees and sale price, and the plan is Agile with CI/CD. Class and ER diagrams are in the case study.

Mobile app | Zero food waste

PulsePantry

Scan a grocery receipt, see what is about to expire, get a recipe that uses it, and log whether it was used or thrown away. UK homes throw away about 4.4 million tonnes of edible food a year (WRAP, 2026); this app is built to shrink that number at home.

Light and dark themes from one token set, text plus icon plus due date on every expiry alert, and an original infographic instead of stock photography. A Figma kit holds the icons, style frames and components.

The core loop is receipt OCR, a shelf-life lookup by food category, expiry alerts, and a waste log that feeds the success metrics. Documented with class and ER diagrams, MVP scope, and an Agile delivery plan.

Desktop dashboard | Energy and AI

DSO Grid Control Agent

A command-centre concept for electricity distribution. An AI agent flags feeder overloads and explains its recommendation; a human operator approves or overrides; every action is written to an audit log.

Three themes (navy and sand, slate, and light) for long control-room shifts, one card grid, and status shown with words and icons as well as colour. My audit found the type too small, so a larger scale is planned.

Flow: feeder, event, AI decision with rationale, Beckn-style dispatch, audit entry. Class and ER diagrams cover feeders, DER resources, decisions and logs. A hybrid Agile and V-model plan suits safety-related software.

Desktop web | Independent concept, not affiliated with YouTube

YouTube redesign: purpose-based modes

One platform, three purposes. Pick Student, Kids or Entertainment and the theme, home feed and search change to fit. Kids mode is protected by a parent PIN that can be created, entered or reset.

The layout stays identical across modes and only mood and content rules change, so nobody has to relearn the product. Cream and sage for study, warm browns for kids, navy for entertainment.

Mode profiles, content filters and a hashed parental PIN are modelled in the class and ER diagrams. A dual-track Agile plan runs discovery alongside delivery with child-safety review gates.

Mobile app | AI-first banking

Ledgerly

An AI co-pilot built into a bank account: it reads spending, forecasts the month ahead and suggests safe money moves, but never moves money without an explicit tap. 11 screens, an 8-round WCAG 2.2 AA audit, and full requirements and user stories, documented the way a product team would.

Warm paper backgrounds, ink-black text and one deep teal accent read as calm and trustworthy; every AI moment carries the same amber sparkle and a plain-English reason, so the assistant explains rather than decides.

Human-in-the-loop by design: the AI can read accounts and propose automations, but every money movement needs explicit user approval. Documented with functional and non-functional requirements, user stories with acceptance criteria, and a full contrast audit (4.55:1 to 16:1 across all 11 screens).
